Equity local Import

A personal research tool. It is not offered as a service.

Equity local Import is a command-line program that its owner runs on their own computer. It copies public financial data from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's EDGAR system into the owner's own Google Sheets research workbooks, so the workbooks can model a company's financial statements.

What it does with your Google account

  • It asks for one permission: drive.file. With it, the program can open only the spreadsheets you choose in Google's file picker during sign-in. It can't see any other file in your Drive.
  • It reads and writes cells in those chosen spreadsheets through the Google Sheets API. It doesn't read your email, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.
  • Everything runs on your own computer. There is no server, and nothing from your Google account is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Who uses it

Only its owner, for their own investment research. It makes no investment decisions and gives no advice.
